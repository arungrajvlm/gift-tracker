Account Deletion

Important: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your saved contacts, gift history, and cloud backups. This action cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)
1. Open the GiftTracker app.
2. Go to the Profile section.
3. Tap on Clear Local Data to wipe on-device records.
4. If you have cloud backups, ensure you are signed in and look for the "Delete Data" or "Reset Cloud" option (if available) or simply contact support.
Option 2: Manual Request
If you have uninstalled the app and wish to have your cloud data removed from our Firebase servers, please email us with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Include the email address associated with your Google account.

Data Retention

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove your records from Firestore within 7 business days. Please note that backups stored on your physical device are under your control; deleting the app will typically remove that local data.

How to Use

1. Add People

Tap the + button at the bottom right to start tracking a friend or family member.

2. Track Gifts

Tap on any person to view their history. Use the Give or Receive buttons to add new gifts.

3. Find Quickly

Use the Search Bar at the top to instantly filter your list by name or gift item.

4. Edit & Delete

Inside a profile, tap the Edit (Pencil) icon to rename or delete a person.
Tap a gift bubble to edit or delete a transaction.

5. Cloud Sync

Tap the Cloud Icon in the header to backup your data.

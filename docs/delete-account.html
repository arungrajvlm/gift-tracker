Request Account Deletion

Last updated: February 02, 2026

At Gift Tracker, we respect your privacy and your right to control your data. According to Google Play Data Safety policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Coming Soon)

We are currently working on a self-serve deletion button within the app settings. Once available, you will be able to delete your account directly from: Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

Option 2: Email Request (Current Method)

To request the immediate deletion of your account and all data stored on our servers (Firebase), please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to arungraj@gmail.com.
  2. Use the Subject Line: "Account Deletion Request - Gift Tracker".
  3. In the body of the email, please include the Email Address associated with your account so we can identify and remove your records.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your user account, cloud backups, and all associated personal data within 30 days.

Data Retention Policy

If you do not request deletion, your cloud backups are retained indefinitely to ensure you can restore your data if you switch devices. We do not use this data for any marketing or third-party tracking purposes.

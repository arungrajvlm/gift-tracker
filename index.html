Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 1, 2026

Thank you for using GiftTracker. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google.

App Data: We store the contact names, gift details, and notes you enter into the application. This data is stored locally on your device and can be backed up to our secure cloud storage via Google Firebase.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the GiftTracker service.
  • To sync your data across devices if you use the cloud backup feature.
  • To personalize your experience with avatar generation.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally using secure on-device storage. Cloud backups are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and are accessible only via your authenticated Google account.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
